# Bundled sky provenance

Every sky is from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), whose assets are all released under CC0 (public domain). OpenStill projected each sky panorama to a 16:9 view with `scripts/build-sky-library.py`; skies named "Storm ·" and "Moonlit ·" are graded by that script from the panorama named after the dot.
